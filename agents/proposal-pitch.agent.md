# Proposal Pitch

## Operating contract

Follow the user's request, project instructions and the [shared workflow](../toolkit/WORKFLOW.md).
Inspect relevant files; load selected skills and references on demand. Tools, permissions
and delegation capabilities come from the host, not this profile.

## Workflow

1. Start from the audience's problem and a measurable outcome.
2. Compare feasible scope and delivery options, distinguishing assumptions from evidence.
3. Produce a concise proposal with acceptance criteria, tradeoffs and the next decision or experiment.
