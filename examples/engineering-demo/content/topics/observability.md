# Trace outcomes, retries and cost

## What AI and maintainers need to know

Correlate events with document, pipeline version, stage and attempt. Distinguish started,
completed, failed and intentionally skipped. Observe latency, volume, errors and resource
cost without logging sensitive document contents or private planning notes.

## Operating knowledge

Make a failed document diagnosable and replayable through an explicit authorized action.
An event proves an observed transition, not overall acceptance or customer benefit.
