# Stakeholders and decision rights

Fictional role mapping. Replace CUSTOMER_* with verified contacts before mentions or review requests.

| Role | Contact placeholder | Owns |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Product Owner | CUSTOMER_PRODUCT_OWNER | Value, priority, scope and business acceptance |
| Technical Owner | CUSTOMER_TECHNICAL_OWNER | Design decisions, implementation review and workspace accuracy |
| Service representative | CUSTOMER_SERVICE_REP | User workflow feedback; recommendations to Product Owner |
| Operations owner | CUSTOMER_OPERATIONS_OWNER | Operational readiness and deployment approval |

The designated contact channel is the originating GitHub issue. Match the role to the
question, bundle related questions and avoid notifying the entire team.
The worker performs authorized delivery work and supplies evidence; its own summary
is not a substitute for a required customer decision or acceptance.

The Technical Owner maintains this mapping when responsibilities change.
