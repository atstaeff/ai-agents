# 05 — Integrate and retain

Use at integration, completion or handoff. Read current review/check evidence, actual
authorization and affected [knowledge](../knowledge/README.md) and [ADRs](../decisions/README.md).

- Integrate only within the project's actual authorization; merge and deployment are
  separate actions. Report pending integration honestly when authorization is absent.
- Update current knowledge from verified behavior. Reconcile decision status, consequences
  and implementation links; preserve old reasons and link authorized replacements.
- Keep acceptance and progress at the issue/PR. Close it only at the agreed completion
  point; preserve useful history. Do not publish private plans, paths or chat transcripts.
- Leave revision, result, checks/limits, blockers, pending writes and one next action
  whenever another person or session must continue.

Result: a reviewable or authorized integrated outcome and recoverable shared knowledge.
Further work follows its actual need through the [process index](README.md).
