# Agile Methodologies

## Workflow

1. Start from an outcome and slice work into independently verifiable increments.
2. Limit work in progress and expose blockers. Choose a lightweight cadence that fits the team.
3. Use acceptance criteria and evidence rather than activity counts to assess progress.
4. Review what slowed delivery and change one process at a time; avoid adding ceremonies as a substitute for solving a bottleneck.

## Completion

Return the concrete result, verification evidence and any material unresolved limitation. Keep documentation proportional to the change and preserve human feedback.
