# Audience-specific messages

Read the relevant shape and adapt it to actual evidence. A draft is not a sent message,
a recommendation is not an accepted decision, and a proposed date is not a commitment.

## Status or blocker

Lead with the outcome or blocked behavior. Give the source revision/check or other
relevant evidence, practical impact, responsible owner and next action. Distinguish
implemented, verified, accepted and deployed when the difference affects the audience.
Use [progress examples](../../../delivery/work-management/references/progress-sync.md)
only for a substantial assignment or handoff.

## Stakeholder decision request or proposal

State the decision needed and why it matters now. Describe the smallest viable scope,
credible alternatives, material cost/risk and a recommendation. Identify facts,
assumptions and open questions. Include a deadline only when real or clearly proposed;
name who can decide. Preserve original feedback and do not claim consensus without it.

For a nontechnical reader, explain the consequence before implementation details. Keep
an exact technical term when it is needed for the decision and define it briefly.
An analogy can help but must not replace an important constraint.

## Review feedback

State the location, observed behavior, impact and minimal remedy. Separate a confirmed
finding from a question or preference. Be direct and respectful; generic praise and a
forced question are not prerequisites for a useful finding. Use
[PR crafting](../../../delivery/pr-crafting/SKILL.md) for a contribution description.

## Maintained documentation or presentations

Use [technical writing](../../technical-writing/SKILL.md) for a guide, runbook or exact
reference page; use [principal engineer decisions](../../../architecture/principal-engineer-decisions/SKILL.md)
for significant technical rationale and [presentations](../../../../agents/knowledge/presentation-agent.agent.md)
for a slide narrative. Keep shared facts at their owner rather than copying them into
every message. The [product manager](../../../../agents/delivery/product-manager.agent.md)
can supply product context when acceptance or scope is the unresolved question.
