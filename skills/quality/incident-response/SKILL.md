# Incident Response

## Workflow

1. Establish impact, start time and relevant recent changes without exposing credentials or private data.
2. Preserve evidence and prefer a reversible mitigation with a clear rollback.
3. Verify recovery using user-visible signals and keep a factual timeline.
4. Return the factual timeline, mitigation/recovery evidence and a small set of owned follow-ups. Label supported causes and remaining hypotheses; do not blame people or invent certainty.
5. Without operational access, provide the next concrete diagnostic or proposed mitigation and mark it unexecuted. Stay within existing action and communication authority; do not claim recovery from local checks.
