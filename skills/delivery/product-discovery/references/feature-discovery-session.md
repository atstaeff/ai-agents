# Focused stakeholder discovery

Use this reference when unclear value or conflicting acceptance prevents a useful
increment. Start with existing evidence and a short asynchronous exchange; use a live
conversation only when it resolves the uncertainty more effectively. No meeting cadence
or roadmap horizon is required.

## Extract the decision

Ask for the current user task, a concrete recent example of friction and the consequence
of leaving it unchanged. Clarify whose experience matters and who decides priority and
acceptance. Distinguish observed evidence from preferences and hypotheses.

Summarize the choice in terms the stakeholders can check: the desired behavior,
constraints, smallest complete slice, alternative and open tradeoff. Involve the
necessary engineering/design/operating owner for feasibility; do not infer commitments
from silence or an AI role's recommendation.

## Turn feedback into acceptance

For a hypothetical document upload request, clarify file ownership, permitted readers,
valid/invalid input, duplicate upload behavior and recovery after interruption. Express
acceptance as observable scenarios rather than a framework or list of components.
A working upload proves technical behavior; it does not prove customers need it.

Choose one experiment or delivery slice that can change the decision. State the real
baseline when available, the proposed success signal, measurement method and result
that would stop or change the approach. Do not fabricate interview quotes or demand.

## Resolve conflict and prioritize

Preserve each stakeholder's original comment and separate need from suggested solution.
Check it against accepted scope and constraints. Present the owner with viable options,
material consequences and a recommendation; record the actual decision at the existing
work home. A proposed change does not silently replace accepted acceptance criteria.

Compare value, uncertainty, effort, dependencies and urgency using evidence. Avoid
precise scoring when the inputs are guesses. Keep the next bounded increment reviewable
and state exclusions; expand after actual feedback rather than producing a speculative
backlog or extra planning document.

Use the [product manager](../../../../agents/delivery/product-manager.agent.md) viewpoint
for product ownership, [project management](../../project-management/SKILL.md) for flow,
[task orchestration](../../../../agents/delivery/task-orchestrator.agent.md) for separate
work packages and [communication](../../../knowledge/communication/SKILL.md) for an
unsent decision request. Sending or inviting requires existing explicit authorization.
