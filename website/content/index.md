    [Explore the catalog](catalog/index.md)

- :material-source-branch: **Deliver with a shared operating model**

    Frame outcomes, decide consequential tradeoffs, verify small increments and preserve

    Connect maintainability, security, recovery and other important qualities to scenarios,
